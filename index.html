Our story

Once we started dating, we never stopped.

We met through Facebook Dating in late March 2023. Wilda was a little hesitant at first—Sean initially received the Google number instead of the real one—but it didn’t take long for us to realize there was something special between us.

Our first date was April 6, 2023, on the Hollywood Boardwalk. It was simple: ice cream, a bunless burger, and hours of conversation. From the beginning, we felt comfortable being ourselves.

Sean was drawn to Wilda’s intelligence, humor, fearlessness, caring spirit, and love for life. Wilda saw Sean’s heart through his faith, his devotion to his children, and the way he prayed for her daughter and every one of her many siblings by name.

What makes our relationship special isn’t that we are perfect or always the same. It’s that we choose each other. We pray, laugh, support one another, and keep learning how to love each other through every season.

On September 20, 2025, during Wilda’s surprise 40th birthday celebration, Sean waited in the back of the venue on one knee and asked her to marry him. Through happy tears, she said yes.

Now we look forward to growing in faith, building our family, traveling, creating memories, and growing old together—as The Walkers.
